## Ministry
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/824cbd83-e15c-4ceb-9093-4747e8545247)

Youth ministry is going well. I don’t have everyone’s names down yet, but engagement has been consistent. During the week of Good Friday, I laid out a full list of priorities and shifted my approach from goal-setting to system-building. The goal is no longer the focus—just executing the system consistently. So far, it’s holding up, even with new events coming in. Trade-offs between tasks do come up, but not frequently.

Weekends are locked down. Church is the core commitment on Sunday, with the occasional social engagement. Saturdays are kept open for rest. That boundary helps keep the week sustainable.

I’ve resumed development on the Bible app I started in Brazil. It will be released for free and built to support Linux, Windows, and macOS. It’s a lightweight, functional tool—meant to serve people who don’t have many good options, especially Linux users.

## Life & Rhythm
Progress continues on the bus conversion. I’ve removed a few seats and started mapping the space. Installed a “disco bed” in the back—it’s a convertible bunk cot that doubles as a couch. Nothing too permanent yet, just testing layout options.

Work has been steady—plenty to do. Every week is pre-planned. The schedule is dense, but resilient. Adjustments don’t break the system. The shift to prioritizing process over outcome has made it easier to keep moving without getting overwhelmed.

At home, we’ve started family workouts. Everyone’s been sore, which has actually made things more fun—mutual pain builds solidarity. I’ve also scheduled in protected family time and downtime. Monday through Friday is packed; weekends are not. That division is intentional and holding up well.
