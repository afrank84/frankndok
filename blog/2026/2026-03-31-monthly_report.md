### Life / Travel / Work / Ministry
Celebrated my Daughter's Baptism Day. We celebrate like we do birthdays every year because it is the "second birth" in Christ that matters most—went out for her favorite food, sushi. At this point, everyone in the Frank family chooses sushi for whatever occasion is being celebrated. Not something easily made at home, so it's one of the few foods worth going out for.

Got to preach at Camp Good News to the youth. Always nice, as I like many of the youth there and it's been great watching them grow up into godly men and women.

As for travel, we tried a new cruise line called Celebrity (X is their logo) and really enjoyed the ship. We don't care too much about the stops—we're there for the food, rest, and service. All three were amazing. There was a guy named Luis who went above and beyond to serve. I asked about some green olives and where to find them as it was Mediterranean night and I hadn't seen green olives for the past few days. Boy, was he quick to help. I ended up with green olives every night. He'd find me and bring a bowl. The bowls kept getting bigger each night, as I think he was testing to see how many I could eat in a sitting. Honestly, my olive habit is very high and I have no problem eating just olives as a main meal, so I was never disappointed. If you don't know, there's always a free library on cruise ships that allows people to donate books and take books from others. We totally exploit that. We bring bags full of Christian books (almost always apologetics) on different subjects that point to Christ, leave them all in the library, and bring back an empty bag.

Speaking of distributing material, I had some coworkers from Europe come over (mostly Netherlands) to the office in Gainesville. There's a guy named Hari, who none of my team had met in person but talked to every day on camera. So my teammates and I were very excited to meet him in person. I brought Bibles and goodie bags. Since I was coming into Gainesville every day for work, I always see homeless folks and like to give kits away that I make. Anyway, I always have extra Bibles on hand and was able to give a few more away at work. I talk about Christ at work, so this is really no surprise to anyone, but some had never owned a Bible before, so that was cool.

In other news, we had a surprise birthday party for Papa at 70. Had some family come from out of town to surprise him and we all ate out and went bowling. Apparently, I do lunges when I bowl, but it worked as I was getting the best score ever. I also learned that lunging for an hour makes it where it's difficult to sit and stand without making noises of pain the next day.

Then got to see some fellow brothers and sisters in Christ over Easter, which was really cool. Our families just hung out together, went to church together, and to my church's Easter picnic. It was a really good day. I don't remember much else as I'm writing all this after the fact.
