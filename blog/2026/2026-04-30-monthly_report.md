### Life / Travel / Work / Ministry

In April, we celebrated the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ and the death of our bees, who only lasted a few weeks. I love bees, but bees don't love me. Between the wildfires 
that brought a ton of smoke into our yard, the carpenter ants trying to take the sugar water, and the beetles that infest hives, we never stood a chance. I will never buy another 
bee hive again, but I will start creating traps for them so I can catch native bee species and help them flourish.

Got to see a movie in theaters recently, which was nice, as there is not much out there that I am willing to leave my house for and pay money to see. However, the Super Mario 2 movie was 
decent, and it helps support our local theater, which keeps prices very reasonable at $5 per person.

Did a road trip up to North Carolina to visit my aunt and uncle, and even got to see some of my cousins and second cousins. During that trip, we also went and saw the 
USMC Museum up in Virginia, and it was probably the nicest museum I have ever seen. Saw some stuff in there about my unit, but nothing major. Met an 0311 (Rifleman) from
Vietnam, and we talked about different tactics the enemy used to try to kill us and laughed about it. He was a really nice guy. There are too few riflemen left, so I am always 
happy to stop and talk to a grunt.

In other news at church, my daughter now sings in a group at church. I call it T-POP (like K-POP in Korea), but it has no name. Just a bunch of girls coming together to sing for 
God's glory. I was going to say a bunch of little girls, but since my daughter is now taller than my wife, I probably shouldn't say little anymore. Also, the T stands for Trinity. 
Anyhow, the church also hosts something called Grand Prix, which is a bunch of racing with wooden cars that the kids take home and work on. Of course, any wooden car my 
kids are going to work on is going to need some electronics wired into it because we are nerds. I helped my son work on his car and make it look like Tron with neon 
red lighting strips that can be turned on and off with a switch. My wife helped my daughter make hers very pink and Hello Kitty with rainbow LEDs.

Then we got invited to go to Busch Gardens with another family in Christ, and it was fun. I do not like theme parks, heat, crowds, waiting in lines... I think you get the idea. 
Anyhow, I made a wonderful bag holder, as between our families it was something like 10 people. I also got a chance to read a book about Brother Andrew (*God's Smuggler*), 
which was enough of a page-turner that I had the entire thing read before I left the park. I was at the park for fellowship and to hold bags, and both were accomplished. 
So, mission success.
