## Travel

We started the month already on the road on our trip to Yellowstone National Park. Along the way, we had a mechanical failure with our rental van that resulted in getting a replacement, but the trip continued.

We saw more corn and soybeans than I thought possible driving through Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, and South Dakota. We stopped at the Corn Palace in South Dakota, which was corny in a good way, and visited multiple national parks and monuments along the way.

In Idaho, we went caving at Craters of the Moon, discovered Arco which is the first city in the world to be powered by atomic energy and visited the Idaho Potato Museum. Because apparently seeing endless fields of crops wasn't enough, we needed museums dedicated to them too.

We also got to celebrate our daughter's birthday while camping. Birthday dinner was a big NY strip steak from one of our new favorite grocery stores we discovered on the trip, Grocery Outlet. 

We made our way through Oregon and California and saw some absolutely massive trees, then found a diamond in the rough at Middlegate Station in Nevada. We slept out on the salt flats in Utah and drove through the incredible scenery of Colorado.

Later, we made it to Wisconsin, where we were able to visit and help some missionary friends. On the final stretch home, we stopped to visit family and made a trip to the Unclaimed Baggage Center.

After thousands of miles, countless sights, a broken rental van, and a whole lot of time on the road, we finally turned in the rental and were very happy to be home.

Naturally, we celebrated with sushi.

That was September.
