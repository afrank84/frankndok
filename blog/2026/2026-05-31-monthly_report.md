### Work/Life
I officially formed a new LLC called DevOpsGG to focus on software and video game development. After spending years working in DevOps, automation, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and systems engineering, I decided to apply the same engineering mindset toward building and shipping games. The current development stack is centered around Godot 4.4.1 with an emphasis on co-op systems, procedural gameplay, and scalable project architecture.

The primary goal of DevOpsGG is execution and completion. Too many projects remain stuck in prototype stages, so the focus is on iterative development, stable systems, version control discipline, tooling, and long-term maintainability while still creating fun gameplay experiences. I also plan to document the technical side of development, including architecture decisions, automation workflows, debugging challenges, and lessons learned throughout the process of building and shipping games.
