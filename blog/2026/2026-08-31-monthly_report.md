## Work

This month we started building a new video game called One More Cast as a family project. I am handling the programming, my wife is helping with the artwork, and the kids have become the game testers.

We only had about 14 days of actual development time before leaving on our trip, but managed to get the game into a demoable state. There is still plenty left to do, including adding more fish and building out the tech tree.

We also ran into a particularly nasty crash that took a while to track down. It ended up being related to hardware acceleration in GTK/WebKit. I finally tracked down and worked around the problem while we were on the road after leaving at the end of the month.
