# 2025-03-Brazil (4 People)


## Purpose  
This mission trip is dedicated to serving and ministering while traveling light, ensuring that every item packed serves a meaningful purpose. The journey will include visiting local churches, fostering relationships with neighbors, and engaging in community outreach. A key focus will be assisting with build projects, providing hands-on support to those in need, and contributing to the well-being of local communities. Additionally, the trip will involve paticipation in Bible studies, equipping believers with a deeper understanding of Scripture, and presenting the gospel through structured teachings and personal conversations. 


### Need Ability to:
- Adapt to weather changes, from wearing a jacket to swimming.  
- Travel light and fit everything into a small rental vehicle.  
- Avoid extra luggage fees.  
- Avoid bringing anything valuable that I can't afford to lose.  
- Be prepared for manual labor while ensuring everything is TSA-compliant.  
- Provide tech support and use technology to help present the gospel.
